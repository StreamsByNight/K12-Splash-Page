Instructure Security Incident — Update

We are aware of the cybersecurity incident affecting Instructure, the provider of our Canvas platform. Canvas is currently down.

Please follow these safety protocols:

  • Do not attempt to log into Canvas
  • Do not click on any suspicious pop-up links
  • Do not respond to messages about private Canvas conversations

We are working with authorities and will provide updates as they become available.
