Instructure Security Incident — Information for Our School Community

We are aware of the cybersecurity incident affecting Instructure, the education technology company that provides Canvas, the learning management platform our school uses. This is an Instructure incident.

Canvas is currently down while we investigate. Please take the following steps:

  • Do not attempt to log into Canvas
  • Do not click on any pop-up links you may have received
  • Do not respond to any messages referencing your private Canvas conversations

We are actively attempting to engage with Instructure and the appropriate authorities and will update this page as information becomes available.

For system status updates: https://www.help.k12.com

For the latest information from Instructure: https://status.instructure.com/
